# Layered editorial starter

Framework-free HTML/CSS/JS with original geometric artwork, a layered hero and
alternating narrative sections. No videos, provider keys, external fonts, CDNs or
backend. Sample identity and copy must be replaced. Links navigate locally; the
contact section is explicit about having no enquiry submission.

The sun, arch and plinth move independently with a short scroll offset. Reduced
motion removes those transforms. Phone layout stacks the composition after copy
and does not rely on hover. Adjust the composition for a real brand; it is a starter,
not an excuse to impose the same design on every website.

MIT, original to Scroll Site Studio. No client artwork or reference screenshots.
Run the skill's serve.py on a copy and check narrow/desktop layout, keyboard links,
depth movement, reduced motion, contrast and the ending before delivery.

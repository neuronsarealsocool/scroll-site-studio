# Architectural landscape journey

Build an architectural property website with independent sky, residence and
foreground planes. Establish a strong static composition before adding motion.
Choose either the warm or violet image family rather than blending conflicting
palettes accidentally. The warm family has eight images and three property-card
photographs; the violet family has seven images and no standalone property cards.
Only the two residence subjects and two foreground subjects have real transparency.
Opaque continuations require deliberate crop/mask blending.

Keep the residence close and clear, with supporting copy and actions low on the
left and slim pill navigation above the decorative transforms. For the split
headline treatment, anchor each inner text edge beside the visible upper facade
with a safety gap. Every opening letter must be readable. Measure the silhouette
through the entire glyph band, including balconies and vegetation; the transparent
image rectangle is not the facade. Cap type size and vertical placement with the
rendered house once its scale reaches its maximum. Viewport-based type must not
continue growing on an ultrawide screen after the house stops growing.

Use different, restrained parallax rates for sky, residence and foreground. Blend
landscape seams without exposed rectangular edges or excessive cloud cover. Keep
the readable arrival separate from any later passing-behind headline effect.
Follow the opening with a warm spacious statement whose phrases gain emphasis
with scroll, a compact principles strip, metrics, three tall framed residence
cards, full-width numbered advisory rows and a darker left-aligned closing scene.
Section entrances, statement emphasis and count-up values are separate behaviours.
Keep them restrained and make final content visible with reduced motion or no JS.

Illustrative properties, numbers and claims must be replaced with verified project
data or labelled as a demo. Never infer a real listing inventory from these images.
Motion libraries are implementation choices. Coordinate frame updates and teardown
if they are used. On mobile, reflow the headline deliberately, reduce parallax,
avoid long pins and keep navigation above the whole scene.

Capture opening/intermediate/exit states in both directions at 1280x720, 1440x900,
1920x1080 and 3440x1271, plus a shallow ultrawide and narrow phone. Check full glyph
visibility, silhouette clearance, safe gutters, image decode, landscape seams,
focus and horizontal overflow. Do not infer exact CSS values from a reference image.

## Project rules

Use this working prompt as written, adapt it, or lock selected elements and combine
the rest with the user's other ideas. Establish the real brand, audience, purpose,
primary action and required functionality. Keep the existing or chosen stack; for
a new project choose a suitable responsive frontend. WordPress and WooCommerce
are optional. Motion is optional unless required by this selected experience.

Default to Replicate for new media, using the bundled media workflow. Confirm the
model inputs and authorized budget before paid jobs. Show newly selected/generated
media in chat and obtain approval before putting it on any public site, preview,
CDN or repository. Reuse explicit approval for unchanged assets. The library host
is asset storage, not automatic approval for every future project's use.

Use real HTML text and semantic controls. Keep content useful before media loads.
Implement keyboard access, visible focus, touch controls, reduced motion, loading
and failure recovery. Test desktop, mobile, short screens and resize. Verify real
actions and label demonstration data. Do not claim a connected backend or measured
performance without testing it. Preserve applicable license notices; hosting a
copy does not grant new rights to brands, fonts or media.

## Scroll-controlled film

Pin a viewport stage inside a bounded runway and map normalized scroll progress to valid media time after metadata loads. Clamp before the final decoded frame. Handle currentTime seeking asynchronously without stacking unbounded requests, retain the previous valid image during a seek, and support reverse scroll without restarting playback. Use playsinline and muted attributes, a real loading poster and an explicit final held frame. Release the stage into subsequent page content so shopping, contact and footer remain reachable. Do not leave an opaque fixed layer covering the rest of the site.

Request a native portrait version when the composition requires it; cropping a landscape video is a separate compromise and should be described as such. Mobile gets its own type scale, navigation and product-grid layout. Do not trap ordinary vertical touch scrolling. For reduced motion and media failure, display an approved still and normal document flow with complete shopping/navigation access. Defer heavy media until needed and suspend rendering while hidden.

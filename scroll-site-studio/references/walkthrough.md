# Continuous journey

Use one coherent route, N sequential legs and no extra aerial connectors unless
the brief asks for a miniature world. Agree a concept plan for unseen rooms. Label
invented geometry; video does not establish a measured building model.

Qualify two adjacent legs before buying a new model's long chain, within budget.
Keep materials/light consistent. Use each previous **rendered** final decoded frame
as the next start; pin the destination where supported with a forward-facing view.
Avoid camera paths through glazing, walls or furniture. Review motion, not only
boundary stills. Preserve raw/encoded files and first/last images.

Alder & Form used arrival → lounge → dining → kitchen → study, with five landscape
and five native portrait clips. Its proposed private wing and upper retreat were
outside scope. Do not silently add every room. Its historical US$4.77 estimate is
not current pricing or a provider balance. No source photos or paid media are bundled.

Use the bounded template for a hero within a normal website; use vendor worldflight
for an entire fixed-stage page. Full-page engines need adaptation to release into
normal content. Short crossfades can soften drift, not prove identical architecture.
